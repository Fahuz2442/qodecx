INQOUT

Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A quick guide to what goes into a website—and the support that can continue after launch.

What goes into building a website?

We can help shape the project from first idea to launch: discovery and content structure, visual design, responsive development, CMS or commerce setup, accessibility checks, performance tuning, testing, and deployment.

Can you help with SEO?

We can set up an SEO-ready foundation: crawlable page structure, metadata, descriptive URLs, sitemap and robots configuration, structured data where appropriate, image optimization, and Search Console handoff. Ongoing content and SEO campaigns can be discussed separately; search rankings are never guaranteed.

What happens after a website goes live?

Launch is the start of the next chapter. Optional care can include updates, backups, uptime and error checks, analytics reviews, small improvements, content changes, and help responding to issues. We agree the scope and response expectations up front.

Do you provide hosting, analytics, and training?

We can configure a suitable hosting or deployment setup, connect privacy-conscious analytics where requested, and walk your team through updating content and using the new site. Third-party subscriptions and hosting fees are agreed separately.

Can one project include apps, software, automation, or IoT?

Yes. We can map the people, devices, data, and systems involved, then plan a connected solution. Each service page outlines its build, launch, and ongoing-care options; the final scope is tailored to your project.

How do we decide what support we need?

We start with how the product will be used and who will maintain it. From there, we can recommend a practical handover, monitoring, maintenance, or improvement plan—without adding services you do not need.
